About Us

About Y2Kesa

Handcrafted with love, designed for you , every loop tells a story.

Y2Kesa was born from a passion to bring unique, handmade crochet pieces to Botswana. We blend nostalgic Y2K aesthetics with the timeless craft of crochet, creating wearable art that feels personal, sustainable, and full of character.
Our Story

What started as a small hobby during lockdown grew into Y2Kesa, a brand dedicated to celebrating crochet as both an art form and a sustainable lifestyle. Every piece is handmade by Kesaobaka and a small team of local artisans.

Our Mission

To empower local crafters, reduce textile waste by using eco friendly yarns, and create timeless, joyful accessories that make you feel connected to the maker behind the product.

Quality & Craftsmanship

Each stitch is carefully inspected. We use premium cotton and acrylic yarns that are soft, durable, and washable. Our pieces are made to last, not to be thrown away.

Sustainability

We minimise waste by using leftover yarn for smaller items like charms and scrunchies. Our packaging is plastic free and recyclable. Y2Kesa is earth conscious.

Community Impact

We collaborate with local women and young artisans, providing fair wages and flexible work. A portion of every sale goes to crochet skill,building workshops.

Custom Orders

Want a unique colour or size? Contact us for custom crochet pieces , from personalised bags to tops. We love bringing your ideas to life.
